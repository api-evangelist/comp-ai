Comp AI provides AI‑driven compliance automation for SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA and GDPR. Their platform automates evidence collection, policy management and audit readiness, offering a dashboard, integrations with over 580 services and a security‑first architecture. Backed by a $34 M Series A, Comp AI serves fast‑growing SaaS teams to achieve compliance faster and at lower cost.

**Areas:** Compliance · Artificial Intelligence · SOC 2 · ISO 27001 · HIPAA · GDPR · Audit Automation · Security

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Comp AI: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
